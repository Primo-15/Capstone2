QuestData - Creates Interactible script (Quest Data)

InventoryManager - Manage/check/hold inventory

QuestDoorInteraction - For interacting the final objective (door) to go to the next map.

QuestLocationTrigger - Works like QuestDoorInteraction, but for Reach Location type

QuestLocationTrigger - for normal quest objective with reach location objectives type.

QuestLogUI - Updates the Quest Log (J button)

QuestManager - Manages the quests

QuestNPCInteraction - Script for NPCs with that is a part of a quest (Can also be applied on objects that a player may interact, example: treasure chest)

QuestSceneTransition - Responsible for transition of Scene to Scene

QuestUITracker - Updates the Quest HUD
